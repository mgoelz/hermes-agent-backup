## Trading / speculation with micro-capital (parked 09/2026)

**What:** Michael offered access to a trading platform + €100 starting capital and asked
how I would multiply it. Follow-ups: Kalshi, then prediction markets generally.

**What killed it:**
- Structural fee drag: at 2 trades/week × ~€1 fee, €104/year in fees on €100 capital
  → needs +104% p.a. just to break even (computed live with execute_code).
- Realistic market return (~7.5% p.a.) ≈ €7.50/year — no meaningful multiplication.
- Kalshi: not available to German/EU residents; workarounds violate platform ToS →
  dies on his hard filter #1 (gray zones). Polymarket geoblocked in DE.
- No verifiable edge: winning at prediction/speculation markets requires being
  consistently better than the market average — special knowledge or quant
  infrastructure, not gut reads. He accepted this reasoning without pushback.

**Outcome / pivot:** Agreed on a free edge-test instead: Manifold (play-money
prediction market, manifold.markets, legal in DE, no KYC). Michael creates the
account (Google/Apple login required — only path; agent must NOT take his Google
credentials; he sends an API key instead, Manifold has a free public API). Plan:
50–100 dated-market bets over 4–6 weeks, fixed stake, each with his estimated
probability + one-line rationale → calibration curve + Brier score vs market
price → only if measurable edge, discuss real-money markets.

**Revival would need:** documented edge on Manifold (calibration + positive Brier
vs market), or a completely different angle (e.g. his Insolvenz data expertise as
forecasting input — noted as a 4-figure idea, not a €100 one).

**Lesson for future pitches:** €100-type capital-multiplication asks → answer with
the fee-drag math first, then redirect to skill/testing-based paths. He responds
to honest kill-math and stays engaged.
