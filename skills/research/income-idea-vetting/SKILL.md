# Income-Idea Vetting (for Michael)

Use when Michael asks for business/income ideas, wants one sharpened
(konkur­renz, risks, effort), or re-opens the 'earn money with agent help'
topic. German language throughout.

## Hard filters (non-negotiable, user-stated)

1. **Nothing verwerflich, no gray zones.** No fake personalization, no
   spam/mass cold outbound, no scraping of personal data beyond clearly
   public/legal sources, no deceptive automation. If an idea needs any of
   that to work, kill it proactively — he checks.
2. **Revenue must be worth it.** Etsy-printable scale (€100–400/month
   after months) was explicitly rejected as 'zu niedrig'. Aim for
   €1,000+/month potential with a credible path.
3. **The agent does ~90% of the work.** His ongoing share: account setup,
   approvals, short calls — hours per week, not days. He will set up
   accounts/subscriptions on request ('wenn du Abos brauchst, richte ich
   die ein') — needing paid tools is fine.
4. **Low fixed budget** (~€50/month region). He rejected high-revenue
   paths that needed real capital; paid SaaS tooling is acceptable.

## Workflow that works

1. **Research before proposing:** web_search what already exists, saturation
   level, and realistic earnings data (prefer honest breakdowns: median
   income, failure rates) — he values being told 'I was too optimistic'.
2. **Sharpen on request, structured:** existing solutions table → revenue
   model (staged: subscription → dossiers → success fees) → risk table →
   effort estimate (agent-hours vs user-hours) → honest kill-criteria.
3. **Prove with a working MVP when cheap:** he responds well to a demo
   built before commitment (the Insolvenz-Radar prototype ran same-session).
4. **Distribution decides, not tech.** Ideas whose acquisition = waiting
   for inbound, or = gray-zone outbound, both die. The viable middle:
   legal-pull markets (compliance deadlines, public registries) + inbound
   lead magnets + agency partnerships.
5. **Project brief for external challenge:** he likes a structured brief
   (idea, data source, model, competition, weaknesses, 90-day plan with
   Go/Pivot thresholds, explicit challenge questions) delivered as a FILE
   in chat, to hand to third parties.

## Pitfalls

- Don't propose platform-mass-products (Etsy/KDP/facel­ess YouTube) —
  saturated, low ceiling; he reads honest earnings data and will reject.
- Don't bury uncertainty. He keeps engaging after 'my earlier claim was
  too optimistic' — credibility is the asset.
- Programmatic-SEO/affiliate content: Google's 2026 core updates deindex
  scaled AI content — treat as dead unless data-moat backed.
