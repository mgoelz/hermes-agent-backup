# Farm-Stay & Short-Trip Sources (Germany, validated 08/2026)

## Regional portals (start here — list real named businesses)

- **landorado.de** — Urlaub auf dem Bauernhof Baden-Württemberg (Landesarbeits-
  gemeinschaft LANDORADO e.V.). ~370 Höfe, filterable by region (Schwarzwald,
  Schwäbische Alb, Allgäu & Oberschwaben, Bodensee...), by type (Bauernhof,
  Biohof, Reiterhof, Kinderhof) and travel dates. Guest ratings shown per Hof.
- **landsichten.de** — Germany-wide Landurlaub portal; per-region pages (e.g.
  /baden-wuerttemberg/schwaebische-alb/). Gastgeber pages at
  /gastgeber/<name>-<ort>-<id>/ — guessing the URL 404s, find via web_search.
- **bauernhofurlaub.de** — BAG Urlaub auf dem Bauernhof (20k+ Höfe). Hof pages
  carry detailed Ausstattung, price structure, DTV classification.
- **Region tourism portals** — e.g. naturerlebnis-hayingen.de lists every
  Hayingen accommodation WITH online-bookable base prices per unit/night.
  Almost every German tourist region has one; search "<region> Unterkünfte".

## Pricing patterns observed (08/2026, BW farm stays)

- **Weekly-only rental** common for classic Bauernhof-Fewos (e.g. Wilmershof,
  Titisee: wochenweise, in summer 14-tägig; Hauptsaison 115–240 €/unit/night).
- **Minimum 5 nights** typical for family-run farm Fewos (e.g. Altschulzenhof,
  Hayingen-Münzdorf: 55–68 qm Fewo 62 €/night for 4–5 pers., cheaper per
  night the longer you stay); in school holidays they may require 7.
- **Group farm (Gruppenferienhof)** = weekend-friendly: per-person/night
  pricing (e.g. Ferienhof Brunner, Hayingen: Erw. 38–45 €, Kinder 0–8 J. 20 €;
  big Spielscheune, Frühstücksservice).
- **Reiterhof/pension** = weekend-friendly but surcharged: 1st night premium
  (e.g. 200 €) + lower follow-up nights (150 €) + Kurzaufenthaltszuschlag
  (~80 €); extra persons only 5 €/day (Härtsfeldhof, Bopfingen — Fewos
  50–110 qm, good for 5-person families).
- Extras priced separately: Ponyspaziergang ~30 €/0.5h, Kinder-Reitprogramm
  330–395 €/10 sessions, Brötchen-/Frühstücksservice optional.

## Tip for short-notice weekend trips

Farm minimum stays are often negotiable by PHONE (many list office hours,
e.g. Mo–Sa 8–12). Recommend a weekend-friendly primary + a fallback in the
same village/region, with phone numbers, so the user can decide with one
call to each.
